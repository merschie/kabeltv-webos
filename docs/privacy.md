## English

**Controller:** Fabian [LAST NAME], [ADDRESS], email: [EMAIL]

KabelBox TV does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to the developer or to third parties.

- **Connections:** The app connects only to the FRITZ!Box router in the user's own home network (default address `fritz.box` or the address entered in the settings) to load channel lists, TV and radio streams and programme information. It does not connect to any server of the developer or any other internet service.
- **Stored data:** Settings (FRITZ!Box address, "status page in home network" switch), the last watched channels and programme information are stored locally on the TV only. They never leave the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.
- **Status page in home network:** Only if this switch is turned on, the app's stream service can be reached by other devices in the home network for troubleshooting. It shows technical information (version, current channel, log), no personal data. It is off by default.
- **No analytics, no ads:** The app contains no tracking, analytics or advertising services and no accounts or sign-in.
- **LG Content Store:** Data collected by LG when downloading and running apps is covered by the privacy policy of LG Electronics.
